Salary Delay Tracker
A simple web page that helps employees check the impact of delayed salaries. Enter your own salary and credit dates and it shows:

Unpaid months and outstanding salary
Interest foregone if the money had stayed in a savings account (editable rate, default 2.5% p.a. as on IDFC FIRST Bank for balances up to ₹3 lakh)

Broken promises: every dated commitment in the company's emails (salary, PF, laptop, welcome kit), checked against your own credit dates
Email log: all 16 emails with date, time, sender and the dates each one promised

How to use:
Open the page (index.html) in any browser, on phone or computer.
In the blue Your salary & outstanding section, enter your net monthly salary and click Apply to all months, or edit each month.
Enter the Credited on date for any month you were paid. Leave it empty if unpaid. If you were part-paid, enter the amount received.
In the Broken promises table, change Mark as to Kept for anything you did receive (for example your laptop or PF details).
Use Share on WhatsApp to send the summary to your group.

Your data
Everything you enter stays in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded or shared with anyone. Use Start blank to clear your entries.

How interest is estimated
unpaid amount × rate × days late ÷ 365
Savings interest is calculated on the daily closing balance. The range compares the earliest and the latest day of your usual pay window. It is an illustration, not a bank statement or a legal claim. Change the rate if yours is different.
